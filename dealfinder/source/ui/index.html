Deal Finder

Facebook Marketplace near you, priced against eBay, Mercari, OfferUp & Amazon

How it works

  1. Enter your ZIP. Auctions and distances are measured from it.
  2. Sign in to eBay to unlock sold prices, the best measure of what things are really worth.
  3. Sign in to Facebook for Marketplace. Logged out you only get ~15 listings.
  4. Search for anything. Every eBay auction within your radius shows the current bid, time left, what it resells for, and your max bid: the most to bid (shipping included) and still profit reselling it. Marketplace listings get an offer to make instead.

Example: a PS5 that usually sells for ~$290 → max bid / offer about $200.

Facebook groups to search

Paste a link to each buy/sell or auction group you're in, one per line (e.g. https://www.facebook.com/groups/123456789). Every search will also look through these groups' posts: comment auctions go on the Auctions tab, regular for-sale posts on the Marketplace tab. You need to be signed in to Facebook here.

Deal alerts

While Deal Finder is open, it re-checks your watched searches every 10 minutes and alerts you when a new listing comes in as a deal.
