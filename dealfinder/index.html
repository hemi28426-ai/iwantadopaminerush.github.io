What it does

One search (or one button) covers everywhere people sell near you.

🔍

Scan for deals

No search word needed: sweeps ~80 searches across everything people sell, on every site, and ranks the best deals as they come in.

🔨

Auctions near you

eBay auctions and K-BID auctions within your radius, with current bid, time left, shipping and K-BID buyer's premium.

👥

Facebook group auctions

Reads comment auctions in your buy/sell groups: starting bid, highest bid in the comments, and when it ends.

📈

Real resale value

Prices each item from eBay's last 6 months of sales, plus Mercari, OfferUp and Amazon. Shows the low, average and high.

💰

Max bid & profit

Tells you the most to bid or offer (about 70% of what it resells for) and what you'd make after eBay fees.

🔔

Deal alerts

Watch any search: it re-checks every 10 minutes and pops up a notification when a new deal is listed.

Setup (2 minutes)

Do these once. After that, just open it and search.

  1. Download and open DealFinder.exe. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway (it's just not code-signed).
  2. Enter your ZIP and pick a radius (default 125 miles).
  3. Click "Sign in to eBay". Unlocks 6-month sold prices, the most accurate way to know what something's worth.
  4. Click "Sign in to Facebook". Shows all Marketplace listings near you (signed out you only see ~15) and turns on group auctions.
  5. Optional: click "FB groups" and paste links to your buy/sell or auction groups, one per line.
  6. Search for something, or hit "Scan for deals". Deals rise to the top; click any card to open the listing.
Heads up: you sign in inside the app yourself; your logins stay on your computer and are never sent anywhere else. The app browses these sites at a normal pace like a person would, but Facebook doesn't officially allow automated tools, so use it at your own risk. Facebook group auctions have no buyer protection: stick to groups with active admins and pay with PayPal Goods & Services.

FAQ

Why is it a download and not a website?

Web browsers don't let a website read Facebook, eBay or K-BID pages for you. The app does that from your own computer, signed in as you. While it's open you can also use it in your normal browser at http://127.0.0.1:47125.

Why does a card say "Not enough data"?

It hasn't found matching sales yet. Each item gets its own eBay lookup in the background, so wait a minute or click "Look up price on eBay" on the card. One-of-a-kind items may stay that way.

What does "check post" mean?

Facebook group auctions are typed however the seller likes. When the app had to guess the high bid or end time, it flags the card so you double-check the post before bidding.

How is "max bid" worked out?

About 70% of what the item usually sells for, minus shipping and any buyer's premium. That leaves room for eBay's ~13% fee and a profit if you resell it.

Does it work on Mac or phones?

Windows only for now.
