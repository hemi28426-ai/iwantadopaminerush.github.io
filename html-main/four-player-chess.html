    Rules
    • Turn order runs clockwise: Red, Blue, Yellow, Green.
    • Pawns promote on the 8th rank, the centre line of the board. You choose a queen, rook, bishop or knight.
    • Castling works on both sides. There is no en passant.
    • A player with no legal move on their turn is out: checkmate if in check, stalemate otherwise.
    • A king left in check can be captured by anyone who moves first.
    • A player who resigns has all their pieces removed from the board.
    • Checkmated or stalemated armies turn grey. They stay as obstacles and can be captured for no points.
    • Points: pawn 1, knight 3, bishop 5, rook 5, queen 9, promoted queen 1, checkmate or king capture 20.
    • Standard chess. White moves first.
    • Castling, en passant and promotion to any piece all work.
    • Checkmate wins. Stalemate, insufficient material and the 50-move rule are draws.
    • Same pieces and moves as normal chess, including castling, en passant and promotion.
    • You only see your own pieces and the squares they can move to. Everything else is hidden.
    • There is no check or checkmate. You can move into danger, and you win by capturing the enemy king.
    • When two people share the screen, the board is hidden between turns so you can pass the device.
